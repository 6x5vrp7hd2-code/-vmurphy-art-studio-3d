Terms & licensing

Clear boundaries for digital files and commissioned Studio Editions.

This is a practical launch draft for the website. It should be reviewed by a qualified UK solicitor before commercial launch.

Launch draft — professional legal review recommended.

These terms are a working business framework, not formal legal advice.

1. Copyright and ownership

Buying a print, framed work, canvas or digital file does not transfer copyright. Copyright and reproduction rights remain with the applicable rights holder unless expressly transferred in writing.

2. Personal-use downloads

A standard digital download is licensed to one purchaser for personal, non-commercial use. It may be printed for the purchaser's own home and used for approved personal stationery and private events. The source file may not be resold, redistributed, uploaded for others or used on products for sale.

3. Commercial licensing

Business use requires a separate written licence identifying the artwork, permitted use, media, quantity or print run, territory, duration and any exclusivity. Any use outside the written scope requires further permission.

4. Studio Editions

Studio Editions are contemporary digital compositions developed by V. Murphy Art Studio. They are not original paintings by V. Murphy. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the customer receives the finished product and/or a defined usage licence rather than ownership of the underlying copyright.

5. Private Studio Editions

A Private Edition means V. Murphy Art Studio will not intentionally add the final approved composition to its normal Studio Editions catalogue or sell that exact final file as a standard edition. Because digital-generation systems can produce visual similarities, absolute worldwide uniqueness cannot be guaranteed unless separately negotiated in a specific written agreement.

6. Customer-supplied photographs

The customer confirms they own or have permission to supply and use any photograph, logo or other reference material submitted. The studio may refuse material where rights or permissions are unclear.

7. Revisions

Included revisions depend on the chosen Studio Edition tier. A revision adjusts the agreed concept; a material change of setting, composition or brief may require a new quotation.

8. Approval, spelling and personalisation

Customers are responsible for checking names, dates and wording at proof stage. Production begins after approval.

9. Colour, cropping and physical products

Screen colours vary. Physical products may require small crop adjustments to suit the selected aspect ratio, frame or canvas. Where appropriate, a proof should be supplied before production.

10. Commercial quotations

Licensing and trade quotations may vary according to scope, print run, duration, territory, prominence, exclusivity, fulfilment and delivery.
