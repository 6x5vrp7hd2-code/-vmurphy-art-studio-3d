V. Murphy Studio Editions

Create something new, without rewriting the past.

Choose the ingredients of a new composition through a guided process. New pieces are Studio Editions — never presented as paintings by V. Murphy.

Important distinction

Original Collection ≠ Studio Editions

The original V. Murphy collection remains historically fixed. Customer-directed digital compositions belong to a separate studio collection so the provenance of the originals remains clear.
