The original collection

Painted for the love of painting.

V. Murphy painted as a long-standing hobby rather than as a commercial artist. The surviving archive is finite: a few dozen works created over years of personal practice. The studio now exists to preserve, catalogue and share that body of work without inventing history around it.

Two clearly separated collections

Original work stays original.

The V. Murphy Original Collection contains only paintings actually created by V. Murphy.

V. Murphy Studio Editions contains new digitally developed compositions created by the studio, including customer-directed pieces. They are never presented as paintings by V. Murphy.
