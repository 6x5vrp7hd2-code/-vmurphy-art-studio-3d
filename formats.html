Ways to own the work

Print, frame, canvas or download.

Choose a finished piece for the wall, a canvas presentation, or a digital file for your own personal creative use.

Fine art paper

Archival-quality prints

Selected works can be reproduced on premium fine art paper in a small range of sizes that suit the proportions of each original. The intention is to preserve the softness, texture and colour of the artwork rather than over-process it.

Canvas

Gallery-wrap canvas options

Canvas gives the artwork a more object-like presence and removes the need for glazing. Different depths and edge treatments can change the character of the finished piece.

20 mm slim wrapA neat, lighter profile for smaller pieces.
30 mm gallery wrapA balanced all-round depth for most rooms.
40 mm deep wrapA stronger architectural profile for larger sizes.
Mirrored edgeThe outer artwork is reflected around the sides.
White edgeA crisp neutral border around the canvas depth.
Black edgeA stronger contemporary finish for bold pieces.

Digital download

Use the artwork in your own personal projects.

A digital-only option can provide a high-resolution file without shipping or framing. It is useful if you want to choose your own printer, paper, frame or size locally.

For personal use, a downloaded image could also be incorporated into menus, invitations, place cards, personal stationery, celebration signage, mood boards or decorative home projects, subject to the licence supplied with the download.

You can also print and frame the image yourself, which gives you complete control over paper, mount, frame and local production costs.

Important: digital downloads are intended for personal use unless a separate commercial licence is offered. Resale, redistribution or use as a standalone commercial product would not be included by default.
